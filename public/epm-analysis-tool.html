Contract Type Reference

Rise/Fall

Simplest binary — payout if the price is higher/lower than entry at expiry. No streak-based edge; each contract is a fresh coin flip on direction.

Call/Put (Vanillas), Turbos

Fixed payout if price is above/below a strike at expiry. Strike distance changes your odds and payout, not the underlying randomness.

Multipliers

Leveraged directional exposure. Stop-out risk scales with leverage — most exposed to a single bad streak.

Accumulators

Payout grows each tick the price stays inside a range; wipes out on exit. Effectively a "how long will this streak of calm continue" bet.

This is a reference, not a recommendation engine — no contract type is "correct" for a given streak, they just express different bets on the same random ticks.
